Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Bracketeer is a free NFL season pick’em board. This page explains what information the site handles and how advertising works. There are no accounts, and we don’t ask for your name, email or any personal details.

Your picks stay on your device

Your picks and settings are saved in your browser’s local storage so they’re here when you come back. They are never sent to our servers, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Advertising

Bracketeer shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, including ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure and improve ads.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent to cookies for advertising before personalized ads are shown.

Data sources and hosting

Game results and betting lines come from the public nflverse data set and are fetched from this site’s own files. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request information such as IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

Bracketeer is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above shows the latest revision. Questions can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/cev64/pickem.

Bracketeer is not affiliated with the NFL.
